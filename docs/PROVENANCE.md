# Demonstration provenance

All synthetic people, institution/campus/course names and UI copy in this repository were newly authored for this implementation. Addresses ending in `.invalid` cannot be live institutional addresses. No private records or external application assets were imported. Fixtures create no charges or production communications.

Original demonstration content and copy may be reused under CC0-1.0. No source-code license is assigned on the owner's behalf. Dependency licenses remain their own: IBM Plex Sans is SIL OFL 1.1, Next.js/React/pgx/otp use their published licenses. Font files are installed from the pinned @fontsource package and bundled locally. Keep original notices when distributing dependencies.

The core increment includes newly authored mathematics, physics, chemistry and biology CSV examples in apps/web/public/demo, original registered-sheet geometry/fixtures and simple PDF/text fixtures authored in the Go tests. These examples and synthetic evaluation fixtures are CC0-1.0. They establish representative content and narrow functional behavior, not a validated educational or real-world OCR dataset. Written-script collections, lesson media and broader evaluation datasets remain required.

The six-question practice kit in `internal/academic/fixtures` and `apps/web/public/samples` was authored for this application (arithmetic, speed, molecular notation and cell membranes), under CC0-1.0. No external paper or sheet was copied. `uv run scripts/build-sample-documents.py` uses pinned ReportLab 5.1.0 to reproduce generic PDFs/CSV/JSON; runtime registered sheets and private kit PDFs are generated in Go. Private kit identity belongs to the selected synthetic assignment and is never placed in public assets. Synthetic scans establish functional behavior only.

The reference-guided UI refinement uses newly authored SVG response-sheet artwork, icons and CSS assessment-cover geometry in `workspace-art.tsx`/`style.css`. Owner-supplied screenshots guided the visual direction; no external logo, photograph, copy, illustration or source asset was reproduced. Original visual artwork follows the demonstration-content CC0-1.0 grant above.

Owner-supplied OMR and environmental-studies images are retained privately for this requested evaluation only; their redistribution rights are unverified and they are not committed or covered by the original-demo CC0 grant. New automated multi-column/image-key fixtures and calibration geometry tests were authored for this application. No external paper is seeded as approved educational content.

The optional inference runtime downloads LightOnAI and Qwen weights from their official Hugging Face repositories at the exact revisions listed in inference/README.md. Both model cards specify Apache-2.0. Weights are not committed, and those model licenses do not change the owner's source-code license. Model-generated transcriptions are retained as reviewable proposals and do not establish source rights or educational correctness.

The bundled `inference/bubble-marks-v1.safetensors` classifier and its generated circle/digit patch dataset were newly authored for this application and released under CC0-1.0 (MODEL-NOTICE.md). No owner image or transcription was used for training. The synthetic holdout and one private real sheet are limited evaluation evidence; they are not a field dataset. The exact weight SHA-256 and training recipe/report are committed.
