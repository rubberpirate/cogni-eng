# Milestone checkpoints

## Trust and organization — building

Runnable increments: opaque sessions, TOTP and recovery codes; invitation/reset/verification mail through a durable local SMTP path; profile/credential editing and private cropped images with lifecycle recovery; institution/campus/course setup; learner admission and verified placement queue; family relationships; teaching responsibilities; membership access changes; protected owner transfer; placement history; audit and invitation recovery UI. The shared OpenAPI contract generates web/native types.

Evidence: Go unit and PostgreSQL integration/race checks; 5 Chromium journeys; strict TypeScript, Next production build and API/web container image builds. See TEST-REPORT.md for exact limits.

Milestone 1 is **not complete**. Required account/support recovery, custom scopes, enrollment imports/reconciliation, full organization lifecycle, privacy/retention, complete localization/accessibility and operational qualification remain open. No requirement has been promoted to verified merely because these checks passed.

Next implementation sequence: close the access/organization gaps, then academic catalog/composition and immutable publication; complete online sitting/grading/release; paper intake/review; learning/tutor/teaching; media/adaptive practice; institution collaboration; commerce/insight; native/offline/release qualification. All remain required; these are dependencies, not optional scope.
